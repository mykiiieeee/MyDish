Bicol Express

Dish Overview

A classic Filipino spicy dish made with pork belly, coconut milk, shrimp paste (bagoong alamang), and plenty of green chili peppers.

Prep Time: 15 mins
Cook Time: 35 mins
Servings: 4-5 people

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (500g) Pork belly (cubed)
  • 1.5 cups Coconut milk (gata)
  • 1/2 cup Coconut cream (kakang gata)
  • 2 tbsp Shrimp paste (bagoong alamang)
  • 6-8 Green finger chilies (siling haba, sliced)
  • 2-3 Thai red bird's eye chilies (optional for extra heat)
  • 1 medium Onion (chopped)
  • 4 cloves Garlic (minced)
  • 1 tbsp Cooking oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Sauté Aromatics: Heat cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté the garlic and onions until translucent and fragrant.
  2. Brown the Pork: Add the cubed pork belly and cook until slightly browned and fat starts to render (about 5-7 minutes).
  3. Add Bagoong: Stir in the shrimp paste (bagoong alamang) and sauté with the pork for another 2 minutes.
  4. Simmer in Coconut Milk: Pour in the coconut milk. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes until the pork is tender.
  5. Add Chilies & Coconut Cream: Add the sliced green and red chilies, then pour in the coconut cream. Simmer uncovered until the sauce thickens and oils out slightly.
  6. Season & Serve: Season with salt and pepper to taste. Remove from heat and serve hot with fresh steamed white rice!

Chef's Tips

  • Reduce Spiciness: Deseed the green chilies or soak them in cold salted water for 30 minutes before cooking.
  • Richer Texture: Using coconut cream at the end creates a rich, thick, and velvety gravy.
  • Best Pairing: Always serve hot alongside fresh steamed white rice!
